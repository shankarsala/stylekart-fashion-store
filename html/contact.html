Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my active StyleKart order?
Once your order is dispatched from our fulfillment center, you will receive an SMS and email notification with a live courier tracking link. You can also view live delivery milestones directly from the Track Orders section.
What is the 14-day return and exchange policy?
We offer a 100% hassle-free 14-day return and exchange window for all unworn garments with original tags attached. Simply request a return online, and our courier partner will pick up the parcel from your doorstep with immediate refund initiation.
Are all products on StyleKart guaranteed 100% authentic?
Yes. StyleKart sources every garment, shoe, and luxury accessory directly from certified manufacturer ateliers, authorized distributors, and vetted brand partners. Every shipment arrives with an official certificate of authenticity.
How do I redeem my promotional coupon code?
During checkout in your Shopping Bag, enter your code (such as STYLEFIRST) into the "Apply Coupons" box on the order summary sidebar and click "Apply" to receive an immediate deduction from your order total.
